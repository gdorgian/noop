# Noop Aura 11.7.0 — experimental iPhone release

This is a **development build**, based on the NOOP 11.7 line. It makes the current Aura iOS interface available for testing, not as a finished health product. The app may have incomplete flows, visual differences from the design reference, and device-specific bugs. Please keep a backup of any local record you care about before trying it.

## In this build

- The Aura iPhone navigation and designed screens are present across sleep, Today, sessions, trends, settings, ages, coach, goals, labs and instrument views.
- Where the app has a supported real data source, Release uses the wearer's record. The sample person and sample measurements used for design screenshots are Debug-only.
- The 20 September [11.7 design handoff](https://github.com/gdorgian/noop/tree/main/docs/design/11.7), including the interactive HTML and engineering notes, is published with the source so the intended interface is inspectable.

## Important limits

- Intraday Charge has no approved ledger yet. It must not be inferred from morning recovery. Some Charge-dependent figures or explanations will therefore be unavailable.
- Body Age and other longitudinal views need sufficient supported data. An unready view is not a measurement.
- Some surfaces are still being wired or verified against the design; an HTML prototype screen does not mean its underlying feature is ready in this build.
- Bluetooth recording, Apple Health write-back, widgets and Live Activities can vary with hardware, permissions and how the IPA is signed. They have not all been verified on the public sideload identity.
- Health and training values are estimates, not diagnoses or medical advice. This project is independent of WHOOP.

## Installing

The attached iOS IPA is **unsigned**. A sideloader such as AltStore or SideStore must sign it for your Apple ID. The package includes the iOS widget extension; your signer must provision the extension and App Group as well as the app. If it cannot, the installation may fail. This is not an App Store release.

Updating in place preserves local data only when the final signed bundle identity stays the same. If you are changing sideloaders or identities, export a backup first. Do not delete an existing installation to troubleshoot without a backup.

The screenshots on the [project page](https://github.com/gdorgian/noop#the-app) are from the iOS Simulator with **Debug-only example data**. They demonstrate appearance, not readings you will receive in Release.
