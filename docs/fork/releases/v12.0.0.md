# Noop Aura 12.0.0

The first non-experimental Aura release for iPhone, built on NOOP 12.0.0. It answers the reports
from the 11.7 experimental build and brings in everything upstream shipped for 12.0.

## Fixed from the reports

- **WHOOP 4.0 live heart rate.** Aura now asks the strap for realtime heart rate while the app is
  open, so a 4.0 shows a live pulse on Today, Breathe and in sessions.
- **Taps that did nothing.** Many rows only answered a tap on their text; the whole row answers now.
- **Navigation.** Swiping back returns to the screen you came from, and to where you were scrolled
  on it. The screen follows your finger during the swipe. Sheets can be dragged down to close.
- **Half-width and misaligned screens.** Wide background glows no longer stretch the layout, so
  pages, sheets and the terms gate sit on the screen as designed.
- **Goals.** A goal set in Aura is now measured from its starting point instead of never moving.
- **Lift.** The first "Log the set" logs the set, the session clock and rest timer run live, and a
  saved lift becomes today's session.
- **Smaller fixes** across Rest, Day, Svea, Labs, Trends and You: alarm copy says only what the
  strap does, the energy figure is never cut off, the body-profile form names its fields, a glass of
  water logged with + counts in Hydration, and more.

## New in Aura

- **Tab bar:** five equal icon buttons on a translucent bar that keeps Aura's colours.
- **No more old NOOP screens.** Pairing, Hydration, Storage, Weekly digest, Trends report (with PDF
  export), Your data fused, Siri and Shortcuts, Shortcuts export, How Noop works and Strap
  limitations now have Aura pages. Weekly digest and Trends report are also linked from Trends.
- **Svea:** hold the mic to speak.
- **Update check:** Settings → Check for updates looks at this repository's releases.

## From upstream NOOP 12.0.0

Everything in [ryanbr/noop 12.0.0](https://github.com/ryanbr/noop/releases), merged where it fits
Aura: Health write-back fixes, the strap log archive, Lift Log timing, coach brief scheduling, and a
session under a minute is no longer saved.

## Important limits

- Intraday Charge has no approved ledger yet, so Aura does not show it, and morning recovery is
  never presented as Charge.
- Body Age and other long views need enough recorded data. An empty view is not a reading.
- The strap's own alarm on a WHOOP 5.0 is still experimental and off unless you allow it.
- Health and training values are estimates, not diagnoses or medical advice. This project is
  independent of WHOOP.

## Installing

The attached iOS IPA is **unsigned**. A sideloader such as AltStore, SideStore or Feather must sign
it for your Apple ID. It includes the widget extension. For widgets and the live heart-rate banner
to appear, your signer must provision the extension and its **App Group** as well as the app;
without the App Group, the app works but widgets do not appear in the widget gallery. This is not
an App Store release.

### Moving from NOOP

Noop Aura installs as a separate app beside NOOP, so it starts empty. To bring your record across:

1. In NOOP, open Settings → Advanced → Backup & restore and export a backup (`.noopbak`).
2. In Noop Aura, open You → Data and permissions → Backup and sync → Restore from a .noopbak, pick
   that file, then quit and reopen Noop Aura.
3. Pair your strap in Noop Aura (You → Your strap → Pair a new strap). A strap stays paired to one
   app at a time, so unpair it in NOOP first if it does not appear.

Updating Noop Aura in place keeps your data only when the signed bundle identity stays the same. If
you change sideloader or Apple ID, export a backup first.

The screenshots on the [project page](https://github.com/gdorgian/noop#the-app) are from the iOS
Simulator with **Debug-only example data**. They show the design, not readings you will receive.
