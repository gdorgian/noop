Trends

The gap was one gap, so it gets one place.

Four engines were already written and had never had a screen. A returning user could read the morning, the night and the training decision better than the old app — and had no way to ask the data a question they did not think of in advance. Act 9 is that question, at the foot of Trends, and not a sixth tab.

T1 · NEW
The index, which is the act Every signal Noop keeps — night, day, effort, body, and the things you log — each with the one number that says whether it moved at the length you asked for. Nothing else in the act has a door until this list exists.
T2 · NEW
One range control, four screens Same position on every screen: under the header, above the first card. A range your data cannot fill is present but dimmed and unpressable. The count of nights actually behind the answer sits under it always — you asked for 90, there are 84, and the answer is the 84.
T3 · NEW
The dossier, computed rather than written Every correlation on the metric screen is a real Pearson coefficient over the aligned days, at the best of three lags, gated at 21 nights and r .22. Blood oxygen has 17 nights, so its dossier says so instead of drawing a fit — that state is in the data, not a toggle.
T4 · NEW
The lag lives in the row Three slots — same day, next morning, two days — and the filled one is where the effect actually lands. Your evening drink shows up tomorrow morning, not tonight is more useful than any ordering, and a ranking that hides it has thrown away its own insight.
T5 · NEW
Colour is ownership A borrowed figure is set in body grey. It gains the screen's hue as your own nights accumulate behind it, and is fully hued only when it is fully yours. That is how dose-response can answer on day one without lying — the treatment says whose number it is before the sentence does.
T6 · NEW
Nothing clears the bar, drawn on purpose Set the effects prop to nothing or learning and the screen still reads as an answer. The uncomfortable state is designed rather than hidden, which is the only way the ranking stays trustworthy when it does find something.
The engines, connected

One 258-night dataset with twelve real gaps drives all four screens. CorrelationEngine feeds the index, the dossier and the overlay; EffectRanker and BehaviorInsights share the effects screen; DoseResponseEngine blends the published figure with yours by weight. Change the range and every number on the screen is recomputed, not relabelled.

Two doors, no tab

In from the foot of trends, where you are already standing when you start wondering — and in from Svea, when she answers a question the engines can answer better. The tab bar stays at five: a tab is for a place you stand every day, and nobody interrogates their data daily.

Explicitly out

Cycle phase stays dark — revisit after launch as a context layer, not a screen. And no causal claim anywhere: the engines write their own plain-language lines, so the copy review is on the engine strings, not on the layout.
