You

You is a person. Everything else is plumbing.

Amber now means needs attention everywhere in the app, so it could not also mean you. This act moves to blush — the one warm hue in the palette that never carries a verdict — and the hero stops counting wear and starts showing your body clock.

T1 · NEW
The hero is a body clock, not a scoreboard A twenty-four hour ring with your sleep window laid into it, your anchor as a tick, your usual wake as a ring, and a live now-marker. It is the only graphic in the app that says nothing about performance — it just shows the shape of your day. The old twelve-months-worn ring and its three stats are gone; wear is a fact for the strap page.
T2 · NEW
Your record, and a + that finally does something Photo, date of birth, sex, height, weight, optional waist, measured maximum, and the two step calibrations — with imperial entry when units are imperial. The + on You opens the four things Noop cannot learn by watching: measurements, photo, birth details, and a lab result to import (which hands straight to Act 8's photo review).
T3 · REBUILT
The strap page, as the hardware page it needed to be Battery as a ring that changes colour the way iOS does, the connection state with its secure-link fact, Sync now and Sync Apple Health as two distinct acts with their own state, sensors priced in battery hours, buzz strength and a find-my-strap, and the strap log with Copy, Save and a daily auto-export. Manage straps is its own page: pair, switch, forget, rename a 4.0.
T4 · NEW
Settings holds everything Noop actually has Nine everyday groups — record, units, appearance, strap, power saving, features, notifications, automations, Svea, backup, about — each written as a switch with a consequence. Nothing from the real app is dropped; the count is the point.
T5 · NEW
The Lab keeps the sharp edges away from the everyday Experimental readings, the protocol probes that write to the strap, and every diagnostic export in one place — each one naming the single thing it is waiting for. The three that persist a flag on the hardware are badged, and turning one off offers to clear it rather than quietly leaving it set.
Swipe to go back

Every screen in this act pops on a rightward swipe, not just on the back button — drag anywhere in the content. Record goes to You, Zones to Record, Manage straps to Your strap, the Lab to Settings.

Night shift, same act

Flip schedule and the body clock rotates: the sleep window lands in daylight, the second core number becomes your anchor rather than a bedtime, and the history groups by shift.
