### 1.1 The step position — kept, and the reason rewritten

Your middle road, and you were right to offer it. The position as written is defensible on
*display* and misleading on *use*, and the screen it lives on is the one screen in the app that
cannot afford to be approximately true.

It is also already contradicted on screen. `ages/health` lists **Step history · Ready · 9 days
recorded** in its inputs card, and `70-copy.md`'s noise thresholds carry `steps 700`. A wearer who
reads the position screen and then opens the health hub finds steps named as a source. That is the
kind of small dishonesty that makes a person doubt the big honest claims.

So the title stands and the reason changes. `you/position`, third denial:

| | String |
| --- | --- |
| Title `[static]`, unchanged | No step count |
| Reason `[static]`, **new** | Ten thousand is a marketing figure from 1965. Your steps feed the spend estimate and nothing else: no tally on a screen, no target to hit. |

What that commits us to, and what it does not:

- **No step figure is ever displayed.** Not on a card, not in a widget, not in a sentence, not in
  Svea's prose, not as a chart axis. The energy card may say what the day cost; it may not say it
  in steps.
- **No step target, ever.** This is the half that `stepGoal` and `stepsBelowUsual` die on (§1.2).
- **`stepsEstimate` as an energy source is now blessed**, and the energy card names its source
  honestly when that is what it read (§3.2). It says *steps* in the source line. That is the input
  being named, not a count being shown.
- The screen's closing line — *if one of them ever arrives, it will arrive with a reason on this
  screen* — is untouched. This is not a reversal, so it does not spend that promise.

Also `plumbing/onboard`'s inline panel and `Noop You - What We Will Not Ask` render the same five
strings. Three files, one string. §8.

### 1.3 Strength — its own act, not a sixth tab

Your question has two halves and they have different answers.

**Act: its own.** Act 3 is one decision a day, and every one of its seven screens is the shape of a
single cardio session — bands, zones, intervals, a duration. Strength brings a persistent library, a
vocabulary Act 3 has no word for (program, set, rep, load, RPE, nineteen muscles), a live screen
with completely different ergonomics, and eight surfaces. Eight inside seven makes Act 3 a drawer
with two subjects and no argument.

So: **Act 10 · The lift.** Eight screens, listed in §3.1.

**Tab: no.** The tab bar stays at five, and this is already settled law — `32-nav-addendum.md`
Change 1: *the effort is one decision a day, not a place to browse, and a sixth tab would say
otherwise.* That argument is not weaker for lifting, it is stronger, because a lift is a session you
are either in or not in.

Acts are not tabs in this shell and never have been — nine acts hang off five tabs today. Act 10
hangs off the same tab Act 3 does, through four doors:

| From | Tap | To |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `effort/session` | Swap → a lift, or the lift lane on the card | `lift/library`, or `lift/live` if a program is picked |
| log sheet (the **+**) | Start a lift — sibling of *Start a session* | `lift/library` |
| `today` session card | when the recommended or running session is a lift | `lift/live` / `lift/detail` |
| `plumbing/history` | a lift row with an identity | `lift/detail` |

All four are tab-level arrivals or pushes that already exist in kind. No new nav machinery, and
`32-nav-addendum.md`'s Change 1 and 2+3 both hold as written.

**One live bar, two contents.** The bar from Change 2+3 is already shell-owned infrastructure. A
lift session uses **that** bar, with its middle slot reading the current exercise and set instead of
elapsed time and heart rate. Do not build a second bar; two bars means two owners and eventually two
running sessions. This is question 4 in §5.

rather than shipping as a worse copy of itself.

Format, per your note: 1024 × 1024 PNG, no alpha, square, one file and one project entry per
alternate.

## 3 · The inventory

### 3.1 Act 10 · The lift — eight screens

Built in this order, because each earns the next. Units: stored in kg, displayed in kg **and** lb,
per your note — the unit follows `plumbing/record`'s existing measure preference, it is not a new
setting.

| | Screen | What it is | States |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `lift/live` | The working screen. Current exercise, its targets, set-by-set entry, rest timer, advance, finish. Used mid-set, one-handed, possibly sweating — so: 44 pt minimum on everything, the number pad never more than one tap away, and the rest timer readable at arm's length. | running · resting · warmup set · no targets (a program line that is just a name) · last set · paused |
| 2 | `lift/library` | Programs: list, open, create, archive. | populated · empty · archived shown |
| 3 | `lift/program` | The editor. Reorder lines, set the six optional targets. | new · editing · a line with no targets · archived, read-only |
| 4 | `lift/detail` | A finished session, read-only. | ordinary · no program · session RPE absent · pre-identity (no chevron, per Change 4) |
| 5 | `lift/edit` | Correcting a finished session after the fact. | editing · a set deleted · committed |
| 6 | `lift/import` | Hevy: file or API, pick and progress. | idle · reading · failed · nothing found |
| 7 | `lift/review` | What was read, before it commits. | candidates · partial · nothing read · committed |
| 8 | `lift/muscles` | Which muscles were worked and their recovery state. Nineteen, in your four groups. | DX-dependent — read · calibrating · absent |

**Every target is optional and that is the normal case**, which is the single most important thing
in your data model for the drawing. `lift/live` is designed around a program line that is nothing
but an exercise name, and the version with all six targets set is the decorated variant — not the
other way round. A screen designed target-first renders six em-dashes for most real users.

**Warmup is a flag, not a convention**, so it is a control on the set row and warmup sets are
excluded from every figure the session states. Silently including them is the kind of wrong number
nobody reports.

`lift/review` is the one I want to talk about before drawing — see question 3 in §5. We have been
here: the lab OCR strips were drawn against rects the parser had already discarded.

### 3.2 Energy — a card on `today`, a detail behind it

Act 2, agreed, and not Act 3. Act 3 is one session; the spend is the whole day, and it belongs next
to Charge where the day's shape already lives.

The position screen already blesses this, in the second denial: *It estimates what you spent,
because that changes a training decision. It will never set a number for you to eat against.* So the
design job is to honour that sentence exactly — spend only, never a target, a deficit, a remaining
allowance, or an arrow pointing at food.

Four things the card states, in reading order: **what it measured · how much of the day it saw ·
whether the model is still learning · where today is heading, as a range.**

State matrix to design, from your enums:

| | Cases |
| --- | --- |
| `source` | appleSplit · strapWornTime · mixed · stepsEstimate · profileOnly |
| `calibration` | off · learning · active · paused |
| `forecast` | unavailable · learning · available |
| `coverage` | 0–1, stated in words at the foot (rule 7), never as a percentage chip |

Not twenty combinations of card — **five designed readings**, each naming its own source, plus the
cold start:

1. **Measured, forecast available** — the full reading. Range, not a point (your field comment is
   right, and `70-copy.md` rule 7 says the interval goes in the sentence).
2. **Measured, forecast learning** — today's total, no projection, and the reason.
3. **Partial coverage** — a total it will stand behind for the hours it saw, and the hours named.
4. **Steps only** — `stepsEstimate`. Names steps as the source; shows no step figure (§1.1).
5. **`profileOnly` + `forecast: unavailable`** — the cold start, and you are right that this is the
   hard one. It has exactly one number: `estimatedBMR24h`, the one figure Noop can always stand
   behind. So the card's whole job is to make one honest modelled number read as a reading rather
   than an apology — which means it is stated flat, with what it is (*modelled from your profile*),
   and the confidence chip from `20-primitives.md` §15 doing the rest. No empty-state illustration,
   no *connect a strap to see more* button.

`totalBurnedSoFar` nil renders as `—` with a reason, never `0` — `70-copy.md` rule 11, which your
field comment already says in its own words.

`day/energy` behind the card: the day's spend over time, basal against active, the unattributed-HR
time stated plainly, and the source and coverage in full.

### 9.1 The session owner — two owners stay, and the bar is already shared

plan comes off the board.

refused, not swapped.

### 11.1 The restore guard — a dialog, and not a red one

A dialog, because it interrupts a choice the wearer has already made: they picked a file. Three
