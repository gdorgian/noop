DONE — WAVE 2, ACT 10 · THE LIFT
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Prototype: "Noop Act 10 - The Lift.dc.html". Eight screens, every state in
the matrix, the bar's lift variant, the session-collision refusal. Recorded in
spec §12, which wins over §3.1 where they differ.

  ROUTE IDS, prefixed, unique across the flat enum, and the same strings the
  QA launch argument uses:

    lift-live · lift-library · lift-program · lift-detail
    lift-edit · lift-import · lift-review · lift-muscles

  THE TAB: Today. A gym session is an effort. Five tabs, unchanged.

  THE FOUR STATES THAT CARRY THE ACT

    · lift-live · bare — the default drawing. A program line that is nothing
      but a name: no prefilled numbers, no six em-dashes. The decorated
      variant carries the progression reason beside the exercise.
    · lift-live · last — the drop set expanding: one planned set as rows
      3a, 3b, 3c, with the cluster note. The case that breaks fixed layouts.
    · lift-review — summary-and-commit, because the file carries no per-set
      provenance, and the screen says so rather than faking checkboxes.
    · lift-library · collision — the refusal. Three parts, no red, no End
      button, neither action styled as the safe one. "Go to it" / "Not now".

  MUSCLES, CORRECTED. 33 muscles in 7 regions, not nineteen in four. The unit
  is credited sets from the merged projection — 1.0 primary, 0.5 secondary,
  stabilisers nothing — and the screen states that arithmetic at the top.
  Group headers come from the region derivation, never the parent chain.
  Work in aura blue, recovery in lavender, never amber.

  SIX MEASUREMENT MODES, one row that changes shape, drawn on lift-program
  with the catalogue counts. No second unit switch in the act.

  ONE BAR, TWO CONTENTS — AND A THIRD STATE. Same 46 pt bar, same four slots,
  same order: the exercise name replaces the workout name, the clock counts the
  set or the rest, and the set counter takes the heart rate's place. Blue while
  worked, neutral while resting, and READY where the rest has run out but the
  next set has not started — the state a drawing forgets.

  THE LOCK SCREEN ACTIVITY, drawn as its own screen (screen: activity) because
  the neutral had to be chosen where it is read: WORKING #17A2E6, RESTING
  #C6CEC9, one opaque value per state. Four cases, including the one where
  neither reps nor weight is known and the numbers slot is absent.

  THE CATALOGUE CREDIT is one line per session, not a field per exercise. It is
  on lift-library always, and on lift-detail when that session used a catalogue
  exercise. Noop does not credit Noop, so the built-in and your-own cases print
  nothing at all.

  PAUSE stops both clocks and holds the part-typed set. It is not "I have left
  the gym"; leaving is Finish. One control, on lift-live — the bar has none.

  ALSO DRAWN: warm-ups excluded from every stated figure and said out loud
  where the count changes; the strap's double-tap taught on lift-live; the
  archived program read-only with no chevron, no grip and no instruction to
  do a thing it does not offer.

WAVE 3, THE MERGED ENGINES GET FACES — ALL FIVE DRAWN
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Prototype: "Noop Act 2 - The Day.dc.html", now at its full 14 screens. One
prop, energyCase, switches the five readings: measured · learning · partial ·
steps · cold.

  3a · THE ENERGY CARD, on today — DRAWN
       "What today has cost", between the day's shape and today's session.
       Four things in reading order: what it measured, the basal-against-active
       split, where the day is heading AS A RANGE, and how much of the day it
       saw — in words at the foot, never a percentage chip. The confidence chip
       sits in the header's trailing slot, in Act 2's own hue, and solid is
       plain.

       THE ONE DECISION WORTH READING: the thin-number answer here is rule 7's
       INTERVAL, not rule 13's withholding. The band widens continuously as
       coverage falls (0.10 + 0.20 x (1 - coverage)), so steps-only shows a
       RANGE where a measured day shows a figure, and the card never publishes
       a point value it cannot stand behind. Only the cold start has no
       measured spend at all, and there the one figure Noop can always stand
       behind — estimatedBMR24h — is stated flat as "about", with the chip
       carrying the reason. §9.2's withholding applies to a thin score; a
       model stated as a model is not one.

       Spend only throughout: no target, no deficit, nothing remaining. A past
       day loses the projection entirely — a forecast for yesterday is not a
       forecast.

  3b · day/energy — Act 2's 14th screen — DRAWN
       The hero and the split, then the day hour by hour: solid columns for
       measured hours, HAIRLINE COLUMNS at modelled basal for the hours it did
       not see. Same idea as the gauge's ghost ticks — a gap is not a zero, and
       drawing it as one would be the lie the whole card is built to avoid.
       Then "What it read" in four rows: source, coverage as a figure, the
       unattributed heart-rate time named plainly, and the forecast's width.

       ONE RULE HOLDS BOTH SURFACES, AND IT IS WHY THE FIGURES MOVED: an
       unmeasured hour contributes NOTHING to the figure and is drawn as a
       hairline at its modelled basal. Coverage, the band and the prose are all
       derived from the same measured-hours count, so no case can claim a gap
       it does not draw or fold a modelled hour into a measured total.
       Measured reads 1,280 over fifteen of sixteen hours; partial reads 910
       over eleven, with the 09:20-13:40 gap drawn and uncounted.
       Em dash and a reason wherever the engine returns nil. The spend-only
       sentence is stated once, here, in the position screen's own words.

  3c · WHAT CHANGED, a section in trends — Act 4 — DRAWN
       Prototype: "Noop Act 4 - The Bigger Picture.dc.html", on "changed":
       settled · alert · calibrating · quiet.

       Placed under the four measured lines and above Showing up, so the screen
       reads: verdict, then measurements, then events, then attendance. ONE
       SHELL for all twelve kinds — leading rule, the fact, the detail, a
       destination — because twelve kinds must not become twelve layouts.
       PRIORITY SORTS AND NOTHING ELSE: tier moves a card up or down and never
       makes one bigger, brighter or badged. healthAlert is the only amber, and
       it sorts first because it is tier 1 rather than because it is amber; it
       names what was measured and where to look, never a condition.

       THE TIER CHECK ENGINEERING ASKED FOR, done. Their warning was that
       dropping a tier-0 kind lets the feed render empty. It does not: the three
       kinds that are always computable — calibrating, statusOverride,
       strapBattery — ALL SURVIVE the cut of six. My tiers, for the build to
       confirm against the engine's table:

         t0  always computable, so the section can never be structurally empty
             calibrating · statusOverride · strapBattery
         t1  act on it today
             healthAlert · restDayNeeded · trainingSuggestion · planDeviation ·
             recoveryRead
         t2  facts about the week
             sleepCatchUp · hrvTrend · bedtimeTarget · milestone

       The two quiet cases are drawn rather than described: "calibrating" is the
       first-week face, with a tier-0 device fact under it proving the section
       fills on day one; "quiet" is the settled week where nothing moved, which
       says so in one card instead of padding itself out.

  3d · THE WEIGHT TREND, in ages/health — Act 6 — DRAWN
       Inline on the Body composition card's Weight row: direction and rate,
       gated twice by 70-copy.md — the 0.5 kg noise threshold AND the six-week
       window. Together they mean MOST WEARERS SEE NO DIRECTION FOR SIX WEEKS,
       so "learning" is this design's normal state, not its empty one: it says
       what it is waiting for rather than drawing a flat line. "steady" is the
       other quiet case — window long enough, movement too small. No goal line,
       no target, no "to go" figure, no projection. The sparkline appears only
       once the window gate is passed, because a six-week line invites exactly
       the reading the gate exists to prevent. The figure IS the series' last
       reading, so the number and the line cannot disagree. On "weightTrend".

  3e · instrument/raw AND instrument/capture — Act 9 — DRAWN
       Reached by a third door on the index. Deliberately the most utilitarian
       pair in Noop — monospaced figures, no editorial, no orb — because that
       is how nobody mistakes it for a feature.

       ARMED IS UNMISSABLE, which was the brief: the card changes ground, the
       dot pulses, the clock doubles in size, and the screen says out loud that
       recording will not stop on its own and will not stop when you leave the
       screen. It names the battery cost in the same breath. Arm and Stop work
       in the prototype. The list is files with sizes, not sessions.

       On capture: duration, channels, rate, samples, dropped, size and source
       as a monospaced table, then a per-channel strip that draws the DROPPED
       samples as a flat stretch — the same rule as the energy chart, a gap
       recorded rather than interpolated across. Export hands a file to the
       share sheet and says that where it goes next is the wearer's decision.
       Three states on "rawState": idle, armed, and none for the first visit.

  3c · "What changed" in trends — Act 4
       Twelve surviving kinds, one card shell, priority sorts rather than
       decorates, healthAlert the only amber, and a calibrating state that is
       the section's honest first week. The engine itself is genuinely new —
       nothing in the tree computes this yet.
       OWED BY ME FIRST: check my twelve against the kinds' priority tiers.
       If a tier-0 kind is in the cut list, that cut becomes a render filter
       rather than a deletion, or the feed can render empty.

  3d · The weight trend, into ages/health's Body composition
       Direction and rate inline, gated by the 0.5 kg noise threshold and the
       six-week window — so most wearers see no direction for six weeks, and
       that is the normal state, not the empty one. No goal, no target, no
       "to go" figure.

  3e · instrument/raw and instrument/capture — Act 9
       Arm, stop, the list of captures, and one capture in full: channels,
       rate, duration, size, export. Armed is unmissable. Allowed to look
       utilitarian — monospaced figures, no editorial, no orb.

WHAT IS LEFT — WAVE 4, SVEA AND THE OUTSIDE OF THE APP
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  4a · SVEA'S FOUR, all inside Act 7's existing screens. No new Svea screen.
       · Voice input in the composer — Hold to speak, four states: idle,
         recording, transcribing, not understood.
       · The daily brief landing in an empty conversation, with a dateline.
         One case only: the engine will not interrupt a chat in progress.
       · Ask about a chart — one affordance, one position, on every chart in
         Acts 2, 4, 6 and 9. Drawn once, placed by rule. The enable row goes
         on consent, not setup, because it puts a picture of your own data on
         the wire.
       · The prompt library — the suggestion strip gains one More
         affordance opening the curated set. Not a screen.

  4b · THE WIDGET SET — one artefact, "Noop Widgets — the Aura set", at true
       widget dimensions, every family with its populated and no-data face
       side by side.
       REVISED SCOPE, from engineering's count: six families ship today, not
       three — glanceable, live HR activity, coach brief, heart rate, stress,
       lift session. A rings widget exists in the target but is not in the
       bundle. So the work is two new families (energy, and the Lift Live
       Activity's three presentations) plus the rings decision, and the
       plumbing/widgets sentence gets its real number only when this lands.
       MY RECOMMENDATION ON RINGS: ship it. It is built.

  4c · THE LIFT LIVE ACTIVITY — Lock Screen, and Dynamic Island compact and
       expanded. All three read the same live session as the bar, all three
       legible at a glance mid-set, all three in the bar's blue-and-neutral.

  4d · THE ICON SET — Titanium (default), Aura (new), and Blued only if the
       source art exists. Two if it does not; Blued retires rather than
       shipping as a worse copy of itself. The Appearance row becomes a
       three-up segmented control and stops saying Later.

  4e · plumbing/widgets' count sentence, and the Trends section heading named
       "What changed" in the build — both deliberately deferred to the wave
       that makes them true.
