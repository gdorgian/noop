THE FIVE ANSWERS
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1.  THE STEP POSITION — keep it, rewrite the reason.
    Your middle road. The position is defensible on display and misleading on
    use, and it is also already contradicted on screen: ages/health lists
    "Step history · Ready · 9 days recorded" in its inputs card, and the copy
    law carries a steps noise threshold of 700. New string, you/position,
    third denial — title unchanged:

      No step count
      Ten thousand is a marketing figure from 1965. Your steps feed the
      spend estimate and nothing else: no tally on a screen, no target
      to hit.

    What it commits us to: no step figure displayed anywhere — no card, no
    widget, no chart axis, no sentence, and not in Svea's prose. No step
    target, ever. stepsEstimate as an energy source is now blessed, and the
    energy card names steps as its source when that is what it read. That is
    the input being named, not a count being shown.

    This is not a reversal, so it does not spend the screen's closing promise.

    Three files render those five strings: Act 5, the What We Will Not Ask
    screen, and onboard's inline panel. One string, three places.


2.  MOMENTUM — twelve of eighteen survive, and the section is renamed.

    KEEP (12)   statusOverride · calibrating · planDeviation · recoveryRead
                restDayNeeded · trainingSuggestion · sleepCatchUp · hrvTrend
                bedtimeTarget · strapBattery · healthAlert · milestone

    CUT (6)     weeklyTrainingGoal · streak · stepGoal · stepsBelowUsual
                weightMilestone · cyclePhase

    Reasons, shortest first: streak is in the copy law's banned vocabulary
    alongside score, badge and achievement — not a judgement call. stepGoal
    and stepsBelowUsual die on "no step target"; stepsBelowUsual needs a count
    to mean anything and a count is the one thing we will not show.
    weightMilestone is a crossed target by another name, against "no weight
    goal". cyclePhase stays dark per the not-in-this-release list.
    weeklyTrainingGoal is a goal mechanic — no published position covers it,
    which is exactly what the positions exist for.

    milestone survives NARROWED to records: a longest, a fastest, a first.
    Never a count of consecutive days, which is a streak wearing a different
    word. healthAlert survives with the most care of the twelve — the only
    kind that may use amber, the only one that may never imply a diagnosis,
    and it must route somewhere.

    The section is not called Momentum. In Aura it is "What changed", which is
    what the twelve surviving kinds all are. The engine keeps its name; the
    heading is ours.


3.  STRENGTH — its own act, reached through Act 3. The tab bar stays at five.

    Your question has two halves with different answers.

    ACT: its own. Act 10 · The lift, eight screens. Act 3 is one decision a
    day and all seven of its screens are the shape of a single cardio session.
    Strength brings a persistent library, a vocabulary Act 3 has no word for
    (program, set, rep, load, RPE, nineteen muscles), and a live screen with
    completely different ergonomics. Eight inside seven makes Act 3 a drawer
    with two subjects and no argument.

    TAB: no — and this is already settled law. Nav addendum Change 1: the
    effort is one decision a day, not a place to browse, and a sixth tab would
    say otherwise. That argument is stronger for lifting, not weaker.

    Acts are not tabs in this shell and never have been — nine acts hang off
    five tabs today. Act 10 hangs off the same tab Act 3 does, four doors:

      effort/session ....... Swap → a lift        → lift/library (or live)
      the + log sheet ...... Start a lift          → lift/library
      today's session card . when it is a lift     → lift/live or lift/detail
      plumbing/history ..... a lift row with an id → lift/detail

    ONE LIVE BAR, TWO CONTENTS. A lift session uses the bar from Change 2+3,
    with its middle slot reading the current exercise and set instead of
    elapsed time and heart rate. Do not build a second bar — two bars means
    two owners and eventually two running sessions. See question 4.


4.  VOICE — the persona gives up the word.

    svea/setup's "How it talks" section has a row labelled Voice with
    Plain · Quiet · Direct. Those are registers. A voice named Plain was
    always slightly wrong, and now it is in the way.

      · the persona row becomes MANNER — same options, same note, same
        behaviour, and "How it talks" still reads correctly above it
      · the mic in the composer is VOICE — control label Voice, action
        "Hold to speak"

    Rejected: calling the input Dictation. Accurate, and Apple's word, which
    is the problem — system jargon in an app whose copy law is plain
    second-person English, and it frames the feature as a property of a text
    field rather than a way to talk to Svea.

    Two places name the persona row: Act 7's setup, and settings → Svea.


5.  ICONS — three, conditional on source art.

      Titanium   the shipping machined face, default        exists
      Blued      the existing blued-titanium alternate      exists
      Aura       the orb on near-black — what the new
                 name refers to                             new

    The alternate-icon feature survives: it works today, so removing it costs
    a real capability to save a file. Two material cuts of one object is a
    material variant; adding Aura makes the set a choice — the object, the
    object treated, and the signal. Three also fits the Appearance row as a
    three-up segmented control, which is the control that row already wants.

    CONDITIONAL, and this is question 5. I can draw Aura to spec. I cannot
    re-render machined or blued titanium — those are material renders, and a
    flat imitation beside the real one would be obvious. If the source art
    exists, three. If not, TWO — Titanium as it is, plus Aura — and Blued
    retires rather than shipping as a worse copy of itself.

    Consequence: App icon comes off the not-in-this-release Later list. The
    other five Appearance treatments stay on it. And the device label becomes
    "Noop Aura" — nine characters with the space, fits at every icon size.

SIX QUESTIONS BACK
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Ordered by what they block. 3 and 4 gate wave 2 drawings — I would rather ask
now than draw against a guess. We have been here: the lab review strips were
drawn against rects the parser had already discarded.

1.  ENERGY — is there a coverage floor?
    Below some completeness figure the day's total stops being a reading. If
    the engine has a floor, the card uses it. If it does not, I will specify
    one and it becomes product logic rather than a design opinion.
    → blocks: the energy card's partial-coverage state (wave 3)

2.  HEVY IMPORT — what does the importer actually produce, sessions or sets?
    And does a candidate keep a reference to its source row in the file?
    review can only show what was read if the parse kept it. If the answer is
    whole sessions with no per-set provenance, lift/review stops being a
    check-each-row screen and becomes summary-and-commit — cheaper to draw,
    but only if I know before I draw it.
    → blocks: lift/review (wave 2)

3.  THE SESSION OWNER — can the shell's owner hold a LIFT session?
    One bar needs one owner. The owner from Change 2+3 was scoped to a cardio
    session's clock, workout, pause state and samples; a lift session is an
    exercise index, a set index, a rest timer and a partially-entered set. If
    that is a field, we are fine. If it is a refactor, say so now — it is the
    largest schedule risk in the plan and the bar's design changes with it.
    → blocks: lift/live and the bar (wave 2). Answer this one first.

4.  ICON SOURCE ART — does it exist for the two titanium faces?
    Three icons if yes, two if no. Nothing else depends on it.
    → blocks: the icon set (wave 4)

5.  THE MOMENTUM FILTER — where does it live?
    A named list, not a switch in a card factory. A cut kind should be
    impossible to reintroduce by a merge that adds a case; otherwise the next
    upstream merge re-enables six kinds quietly and nobody sees it until a
    wearer does. Also: a cut kind must not count — not toward the section's
    badge, not toward its empty state. Six filtered cards must not render as
    "six things happened" with nothing under it.
    → blocks: nothing, but it decides whether decision 2 holds past the next
      merge. If any surviving kind computes off a cut one — planDeviation off
      weeklyTrainingGoal is the likely pair — then the cut is a filter at
      render, not a deletion. Tell me and the plan says so.

6.  UNITS — does a measure preference already exist to read?
    The lift screens display kg and lb and I have them following
    plumbing/record's existing measure preference rather than adding a
    setting. Confirm that preference is readable from a screen that is not
    record.
    → blocks: nothing. Cheap to answer.

One warning, on the piece with the most room to go wrong. In your data model
every program target is optional, and a program line that is nothing but an
exercise name is the normal case, not an edge case. lift/live is being drawn
around that line, with all six targets set as the decorated variant. A screen
designed target-first renders six em-dashes for most real users — so please do
not start the live screen off the model before the drawing lands.
