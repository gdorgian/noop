4A · SVEA'S FOUR — all inside Act 7, no new Svea screen
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Prototype: "Noop Act 7 - Svea.dc.html". Props: screen (six now, chartAsk
added), micState (idle · recording · transcribing · unclear).

  VOICE INPUT — one composer bar, four states, and the audio never becomes a
  file. Hold the mic; speech becomes text ON THE PHONE; what goes out is the
  sentence, after the wearer has read it. Each state carries the line that
  makes that true rather than a privacy screen one tap away.
  A hold under 0.7s lands in "I did not catch that", which is where the
  instruction lives: hold it down while you speak, a tap is too short to hear
  anything. The release is caught on the window, not on the button — by the
  time the finger lifts, the button under it is a different element.

  THE DAILY BRIEF — a dateline, then the brief, INSIDE the conversation
  rather than bolted above it, with an arrival line under it. One case is
  drawn because one case exists: it lands into an empty thread. Mid-question
  it waits, and it does not badge itself while waiting. With manner off the
  dateline is still there and the arrival line says why nothing came.

  ASK ABOUT A CHART — one affordance, one position: the chart card's footer,
  trailing edge, after the provenance line. On every chart in Acts 2, 4, 6
  and 9 and nowhere else; drawn once in Act 7's notes column with its
  placement and stroke, then placed by rule.
  It attaches the SERIES, not a picture — the values, their dates, the unit —
  which is why the enable row went on consent and not setup. It is now the
  EIGHTH GRANT: "Charts you ask about". Every "of 7" in the act is "of 8",
  and the gate's three facts say eight grants.
  OFF IS ABSENCE, NOT GREY. The chartAsk screen draws both: the attached-chart
  answer, and the state where the grant is off and the control is simply not
  on any chart. Noop does not draw a door it will not open.

  THE PROMPT LIBRARY — the suggestion strip gains one More affordance opening
  a curated sheet: eleven prompts in four groups, written by Noop, identical
  for everyone, chosen without reading anything of the wearer's. The sheet
  says so.
  A TAP WRITES, IT DOES NOT SEND. The prompt lands in the composer; the
  wearer still decides. The library is a way to write a question, not a menu
  of answers. Two of the eleven are answered in the drawing — the lift call
  and last night's brief.

4B · THE WIDGET SET — "Noop Widgets — the Aura set"
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Artefact: "Noop Widgets - the Aura set.dc.html". Eight families, each drawn
twice — populated, and with nothing to show. Tweaks: wallpaper (warm, dark,
light) because the set has to hold on any of them.

  DIMENSIONS ARE REAL. 430 pt device scale: small 170 × 170, medium
  364 × 170. On a 393 pt phone the same widgets are 158 and 338 wide, so no
  layout is pinned to a width and the one figure per widget shrinks last.

  THE SIX THAT SHIP, redrawn to the rules: glanceable, live heart rate, the
  coach brief, resting pulse, stress, lift session.

  THE TWO ADDITIONS

    · ENERGY (medium) — wave 3a reduced to what survives at widget size: the
      cost so far, the basal-against-active split, and where the day is
      heading AS A RANGE. Spend only: no target, no deficit, nothing
      remaining. Coverage is words at the foot, never a percentage chip.
      Its empty face is the cold start, and it is the interesting one: no
      measured spend exists, so it states the single figure Noop can always
      stand behind — the modelled basal day — flat, as "about", with the chip
      carrying the reason and the projection absent because there is nothing
      to project from.
    · RINGS (small) — charge, effort and sleep, the only place all three are
      seen at once. MY RECOMMENDATION: SHIP IT. It is built and registered
      and it is the cheapest thing in the wave. The empty face resolves the
      question that was actually open: an axis with nothing logged is drawn
      HAIRLINE, not at zero, because a closed-to-nothing ring reads as a
      failure the wearer did not earn. Same rule as the gauge's ghost ticks.

  THE EMPTY FACE IS THE SET'S TEST. Never a zero, never a flat line, never a
  cheerful prompt — an em dash and the reason for it, and where a gap exists
  the series stops rather than interpolating across it.

  NOT IN THIS WAVE: the accessory families (Lock Screen circular,
  rectangular, inline). Three of the eight would reduce cleanly — resting
  pulse, energy, rings — and five would not. That is a decision worth taking
  on its own rather than inside this one.

4C · THE LIFT LIVE ACTIVITY
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Artefact: "Noop Lift Live Activity.dc.html". Four presentations, four states,
one state table — drawn the way the build should read it: from one live
session object, not four that happen to agree.

  PRESENTATIONS: Lock Screen and banner (364 pt, wave 2's card unchanged),
  Dynamic Island compact (leading ≤ 44 pt, trailing ≤ 56 pt, 37 pt tall),
  expanded (364 × 172, leading/trailing/bottom), and minimal (~24 pt).

  PAUSED IS THE STATE THE WAVE-2 MATRIX WAS MISSING, and it follows from a
  decision already taken: if pause stops both clocks, the activity cannot
  keep counting. It FREEZES at its held value in the app's disabled ink
  (#7F8A85). It does not hide the clock — hiding it reads as a reset, and a
  wearer who thinks the set timer reset will redo the set.

  THE INKS, unchanged and opaque: #17A2E6 worked, #C6CEC9 resting. READY
  keeps the working ink because the next set is the thing to act on.
  ONE DELIBERATE DIVERGENCE FROM THE BAR: the bar's resting dot is #8FD3F5
  and the activity's is #C6CEC9. The bar sits on the app's own ground; the
  activity sits on somebody's photograph and takes the flattest legible
  neutral in the palette. That is the only divergence in the set.

  THE ACTIVITY CARRIES NO CONTROLS. No pause, no next set, no finish. Pause
  lives on lift-live, exactly as the bar has none; a Finish button an inch
  from a Lock Screen swipe is a lost session. A tap opens lift-live, where
  every one of those decisions is already drawn.

  ALSO HELD: the numbers slot is ABSENT where neither reps nor weight is
  known — not a pair of dashes, and the status line takes the width. The
  exercise name takes the ellipsis; the clock and the set counter never do.
  The minimal slot carries a state and nothing else — four states is already
  its ceiling, and a two-glyph clock at 24 pt is a smudge that looks like
  data.

4D · THE ICON SET — FIVE ICONS
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Artefact: "Noop Icon Set.dc.html". The row itself is drawn in situ in
"Noop Act 5 - The Plumbing.dc.html" → settings → Appearance.

  THE RULE HAD TO GROW. The set began as one mark in three materials. Four
  of the five are now that mark — three varying by material, one by WEIGHT —
  and the fifth is not it at all, so "one mark, three grounds" is no longer
  what holds the set together. The larger rule is the real one:
  EVERY ICON IS SOMETHING THAT ALREADY EXISTS INSIDE THE APP. The arc is the
  gauge. The orb is the coach. An idea with no home in the product does not
  qualify, and that is the whole gate.

  THE INSTRUMENT FAMILY — Titanium, Aura, Blued. One mark: the gauge's arc,
  284° of it so the 76° GAP IS CENTRED ON THE FOOT, and one reading just
  inside the arc's end. The ground is the only variable across the three.
  Specified at the 1024 master, 60 pt Home Screen and 29 pt Settings row,
  with a stroke floor of 2 pt — at 29 pt a hairline arc becomes a grey
  smudge at the one size everybody sees.
  The arc's middle is MASKED, not repainted: a second disc painted in the
  ground colour carries its own gradient highlight, and that seam would ship
  into the export.

  BREATHE (new) — the today orb with the pacer's ring around it, frozen at
  the top of an inhale. Breathe is five screens deep (breathe, bcatalog,
  bplayer, bsweep, bfound), reached by long-pressing that orb, and this is
  the only icon in the set that opens onto something you DO rather than
  something you read.
  THE PACE RING IS CLOSED AND THIN, against the instrument's open and heavy
  one: a reading is never finished, a breath is a cycle that completes.
  That difference is the only thing keeping it from reading as a fourth
  instrument at 29 pt.
  TWO ORBS WORK because they differ on three axes at once — hue (cyan
  against periwinkle), shape (a true circle against Svea's blob) and
  structure (a closed ring against a soft halo). Any one alone would fail.

  CAST IS OUT. It was a formal exercise — the mark poured solid rather than
  cut — and it looked like one. Its cost is worth recording: Cast was the
  set's second pale tile, so TITANIUM IS NOW THE ONLY PALE ICON. A wearer on
  a light wallpaper has exactly one that holds, and it is the default.

  THE MONOGRAM IS OUT. A letterform was the wrong instinct. An N in a ring
  is what a product reaches for when its mark cannot carry the tile alone;
  ours can, and the initial inside it was an admission the other four do not
  make. NO TEXT IN THE TILE, at any size — no letter, no monogram, no
  wordmark. The label under a Home Screen icon is already the app's name,
  and printing it twice, or reducing it to an initial, is the tell of an
  icon that does not trust its mark.

  ORB (new) — Svea on the Home Screen, for the wearer whose Noop is mostly
  the coach. EXPORTED FROM ACT 7'S ORB, not redrawn: same silhouette, same
  gradient stops, same bloom. Two orbs that drift apart by a redraw is worse
  than not shipping the icon. Periwinkle stays periwinkle, as everywhere.

  BLUED is still conditional. It ships only if the 1024 source art turns up;
  if it does not, BLUED RETIRES and the row is four. A ground rebuilt from a
  screenshot would be a worse Blued wearing its own name. The sheet's
  "Blued art exists" tweak draws the row both ways.

  THE SET SPLITS BY GROUND, which is what a picker is actually for:
  Titanium is pale and wants a dark wallpaper behind it; Aura, Navy, Breathe
  and the Orb are dark and hold against a pale one.

  TITANIUM STAYS THE DEFAULT — not on contrast. It is the default because it
  matches the hardware, and a wearer who never opens Appearance should have
  the icon that looks like the thing on their wrist. FIVE IS THE CEILING —
  past that the row stops being a choice and becomes a gallery.

  THE APPEARANCE ROW STOPS SAYING LATER. It is now a picker: 46 pt tiles in
  a 44 pt target, three across wrapping to a second line, the name under
  each, and the picked one ringed in aura blue rather than ticked — a tick
  on an icon reads as a badge on the icon. It works in the prototype and
  writes to the same preference the rest of the group uses. One line under
  it says iOS owns the alert and the Home Screen may take a moment, so the
  flash is not a mystery.

  ONE LETTER IS STILL NOT A MARK. See CAST above: nothing in the set carries
  a character, an initial or the word Noop at any size.

20 SEPTEMBER — THE LAST TWO ANSWERS, AND WHAT THEY COST
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

THE TERMS
  Markers moved to 07 and 08. The §7 rewrite replaced the gate's four
  ticks with the document's own four, which removed design's "I have
  read these terms" tick — worth noticing, because it was the one tick
  that was ours and it did not survive contact with the real document.
  Closing note is in, unnumbered. Rename applied to the terms screen and
  the first-launch gate only; the rest of the app still says Noop, and
  that is a question, not an omission.

THE ICONS
  Two of my five numbers were wrong, and both were mine rather than the
  script's. The gap is at twelve o'clock, not tilted 23°, and the ink is
  302° rather than 280.5°. The tilt had a paragraph of reasoning behind
  it about the mark "not reading as a symmetrical logo." The mark is
  symmetrical. The reasoning is deleted.

  The lesson is cheap and worth writing down: I measured a PNG when a
  generator existed. Radius and stroke came out right because they are
  easy to measure across a midline; the two angular numbers came out
  wrong because an angular scan of an anti-aliased arc is a guess with
  decimal places on it. Ask for the constants first.

  Caps are round now, at half the stroke, so the opening draws at 33.9°
  rather than the 58° between cap centres. Titanium and Aura take the
  gold's sweep structure in their own inks.

WHAT IS LEFT
  The live activity's four presentations — Lock Screen, the three
  Dynamic Island states, and the in-app bar — are the next drawing.
  Two parked decisions are still parked: the caption contrast pass
  across all acts, and the five accessory widget families that do not
  reduce cleanly to the gauge mark.

20 SEPTEMBER — FOUR DECISIONS BACK, AND WAVE 5 STARTED
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

THE RENAME — TERMS AND GATE ONLY
  Settled. The app stays Noop; the document and the first-launch gate say
  Noop Aura. That divergence is now deliberate rather than partial, and
  the handoff says so to engineering.

THE ACCESSORY MARK — CLOSED RING, NO CORE
  Settled, and it collides with a rule this set states out loud: "open
  ring reads, closed ring acts." The collision is fine and needs to be
  written down rather than quietly allowed.

  The open/closed distinction is what separates the INSTRUMENT from
  BREATHE inside the app icon set — two tiles a wearer sees side by side
  in Appearance, where the difference has to survive 29 pt. An accessory
  complication is monochrome, ~18 pt, and never appears next to Breathe.
  Nothing is there to confuse it with, so the distinction has no work to
  do and the core — which is the first thing to go to mush — comes out.

  The rule is therefore scoped, not broken: it governs the app icon set.
  The accessory mark is a closed ring with no core, and it is not a
  member of that set. To be drawn with the five accessory families.

NEXT, AND NOW DRAWN — NOOPLiveActivity
  Note for the record: the live activity still open was the HEART RATE
  one, not the lift. The lift activity was finished in wave 4C.

  Noop Live Heart Rate Activity.dc.html. Sibling of the lift sheet —
  same four slots, same inks, no controls — built on one fact the lift
  activity does not have to deal with: a live activity gets an UPDATE
  BUDGET, not a stream.

  What follows from that, and what the sheet argues:
    · No sparkline. Four points a minute drawn as thirty seconds of
      line is an invention shaped like a measurement.
    · Every reading carries its age. A bare number is a claim about
      right now that a budgeted update cannot support.
    · No reading means NO FIGURE — not greyed, not dashed. Same rule as
      the lift's numbers slot, and the same reason.
    · One amber, over the ceiling only, and gated on the wearer having
      turned the ceiling on. Drawn as a tweak so it can be seen absent.
    · Minimal has THREE faces, not four: resting and working are the
      same answer to the only question that slot can ask.

  bpm comes off the activity, per engineering, so this figure and the
  today orb's are one value read once.

THE ACCESSORY FAMILIES — DRAWN
  Noop Accessory Widgets.dc.html.

  The brief was "the five that do not reduce to the gauge mark", and the
  real constraint turned out not to be size. It is that accessory widgets
  render MONOCHROME. All three of the set's colour rules die at once:
  amber stops meaning needs-attention, periwinkle stops marking a
  sentence a model wrote, and blue stops meaning live. Every one of them
  has to become a word or leave.

  Ten placements of fifteen are filled. Five are REFUSED IN WRITING with
  the reason, because a refused placement that isn't written down comes
  back as a bug report:
    · Glanceable      circular declined — two facts, and ranking the
                      night above the day IS the design
    · Coach brief     circular and inline declined — prose that might be
                      wrong needs its caveat beside it
    · Stress          circular declined — "Settled" is seven glyphs in a
                      four-glyph slot, and abbreviating a state the
                      wearer has to trust is lying politely
    · Lift session    circular declined — a progress ring at 76 pt now
                      reads as the app's own accessory mark

  Resting pulse is the only one of the five that fills all three.

  The coach brief's label costs one of its four lines and is not
  optional. An unmarked generated sentence on a Lock Screen is the worst
  thing this app could ship.

THE CAPTION CONTRAST PASS — DONE, AND IT WAS WORSE THAN REPORTED
  #57605C on #0A0C0B is 3.0:1, not "near 4.5". It was carrying 11 px
  captions in thirty files. Roughly 250 instances now use #7F8A85
  (5.5:1), which was already the tertiary ink in most of those files —
  so the pass SHORTENED the ramp rather than adding to it. That is the
  better outcome: the faintest ink is gone because it never passed.

  Kept deliberately:
    · The row chevron's 1.5–1.6 pt stroke. A graphical object, fine at
      3:1. Renamed chevronDim so nobody reaches for it as type again.
    · Act 9 (scope) — its #57605C sits on white, where it is 6.5:1.

  Four SVG axis labels at 10 px were also carrying it and were missed by
  the first sweep, because they set fill= rather than color=. Fixed.

  Tokens updated at the source: NoopPalette.swift, 10-tokens.md,
  12-palette-bindings.md, the build document and the Act 10 coder pack
  all now say the value is retired for type. Dated handoff folders and
  export/ are left alone — they are snapshots, not live.

WHAT REMAINS
  Nothing on design's side of 11.7. Six build-time items sit with
