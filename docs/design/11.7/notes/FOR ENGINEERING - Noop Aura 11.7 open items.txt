C. THE FIVE ICON NUMBERS — CHECKED AGAINST make_icon.py, TWO WERE WRONG
   Your constants are now the drawing. The two that did not match:

     WHAT                 DESIGN HAD        SCRIPT      NOW
     Gap centre           337° (23° off)    12 o'clock  12 o'clock
     Ink                  280.5° / 77.9%    302° /84%   302°
     Ring outer radius    0.39              0.39        unchanged

   Both errors came from design's own angular scan of the PNG, not from

   generator, a different ring and its gap elsewhere, so it is not the
   family mark. It stays archived, which is the decision already made.

   AppChangelog.swift, 278 releases. Confirmed flat by version with year

   Lift the card-kind set out of the factory switch into one named,
   explicit list. Confirmed NOT month-grouped: the 243-in-one-month

   A cut card kind must not be able to return as a merged switch case.

3. THE WIDGET COUNT — BIND THE NUMBER TO THE REGISTRY
   settings → plumbing → Widgets, two places. Design has EIGHT, named:
   Glanceable · Live heart rate · The coach brief · Resting pulse ·
   Stress · Lift session · Energy (new) · Rings (new). Resting pulse is
   confirmed a new build, not a rename. If the bundle registers a
   different set, the list changes, not just the digit.

4. THE ORB ICON — EXPORT IT, DO NOT RE-DRAW IT
   Orb and Breathe both render from the app's own orb source, frozen:
   Breathe at the top of an inhale, so the pace ring is at its widest.
   If they cannot share a source with Act 7's orb, design ships four
   icons rather than two orbs that drift.

5. THE LIVE ACTIVITY — ONE SESSION OBJECT, NOT FOUR CLOCKS
   Lock Screen card, three Dynamic Island presentations, in-app bar on
   lift-live: one live session object, one tick. bpm comes off the live
   activity, confirmed.
   · PAUSED FREEZES, IT DOES NOT HIDE — held value, disabled ink #7F8A85
   · the numbers slot is ABSENT where neither reps nor weight is known
   · the exercise name takes the ellipsis; clock and set counter never do
   · NO controls on the activity. Deliberate, not an oversight.
   All four lift presentations are drawn — Noop Lift Live Activity.dc.html.

6. THE HEART RATE ACTIVITY — NOOPLiveActivity, NOW DRAWN
   Noop Live Heart Rate Activity.dc.html. Four presentations, same four
   slots and same inks as the lift activity, no controls.
   Three things the build has to honour, all of them consequences of the
   activity having an update BUDGET rather than a stream:
   · EVERY READING CARRIES ITS AGE. Seconds, then minutes. Never a bare
     figure — a bare figure claims a freshness the budget cannot supply.
   · NO READING MEANS NO FIGURE. The element is not rendered and the
     status line takes the width with the last value and its time. Not
     greyed, not dashed, not held at the old number.
   · NO SPARKLINE, at any size. If a trace is wanted later it needs real
     sample density, and that conversation starts with the density.
   The amber over-ceiling face renders ONLY when the wearer has the
   ceiling switched on. Gate it on the setting, not on the value.
   And confirm the figure is the activity's own value — same one the
   today orb reads. Two reads of the stream drift silently.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ONE PALETTE CHANGE YOU NEED TO PICK UP
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

NoopPalette.textDim (#57605C) IS RETIRED FOR TYPE. It measures 3.0:1 on
surfaceBase and it was carrying 11 pt captions across thirty files. Every
caption that used it now uses textQuiet (#7F8A85, 5.5:1).

The value survives under a NEW NAME — chevronDim — for the row chevron's
stroke only, which is a graphical object and fine at 3:1.

So: textDim no longer exists. If the codebase references it for type,
that is the site of a contrast failure, not a missing token. The drawings
and 10-tokens.md, 12-palette-bindings.md and NoopPalette.swift all agree
as of 20 September.

Questions on any of the above go back to design rather than getting
resolved in code — most of them look like copy questions and are
actually decisions.
