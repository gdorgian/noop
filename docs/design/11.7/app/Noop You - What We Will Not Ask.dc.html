A position gets
a permanent address

The What Noop will not ask you panel exists only at first run. The two candidate fixes were a you row that re-enters the wizard, or the panel lifted onto its own screen. It is the screen.

A wizard is a one-way road with an exit at the end. Re-entering it to read one panel drops someone on step 1 behind a primary button that marches them back toward pairing a strap they already paired. Nobody does that twice.

The thing that made this easy

you already ends with the panel, compressed into a trailing sentence nobody reads: “…There is no weight goal, no step target and no daily score, because none of them would change what it says to you.” So this is not a new row bolted onto a full screen. The sentence becomes the door — and the note above it shortens to the half that is actually about the record.
