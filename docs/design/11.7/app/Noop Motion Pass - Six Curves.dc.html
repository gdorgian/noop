Why tick is one of the six

Because the live bar has a clock in it, and easing a clock is a bug. Anything that represents elapsed time or accumulated quantity moves linearly or it lies: an eased second-hand runs fast in the middle. The list is short and absolute — the elapsed numeral, the import progress fill, the charge drain, the breathing pacer.

The one that will get argued about

Today’s session card has a chevron and does not push. Both doors into Act 3 are tab-level arrivals — the addendum is explicit, and effort/session has no back map entry. So the card cannot use the push curve, or back will lie from four of five tabs.

It crossfades on swap, and the tab bar’s tint travels to Act 3’s amber over the same 200 ms. That tint move is what makes a crossfade read as you changed place rather than as a dropped frame. Ship the crossfade without it and this feels broken — it is one property, and it is not optional.
