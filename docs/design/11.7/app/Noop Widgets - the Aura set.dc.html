ONE AXIS WITH NOTHING
STILL OPEN What this sheet does not settle
The count sentence on plumbing/widgets is bound to a registry, not typed — engineering has agreed one registry the settings row, the widgets screen and the bundle all read, with a test that fails when the bundle and the registry disagree. Eight families is what this sheet draws and what the registry should hold: six registered today, two to build. The lift session's live presentations — Lock Screen, Dynamic Island compact and expanded — are not widgets and are not here. They are wave 4c, drawn in Noop Lift Live Activity, against the same blue-and-neutral the bar uses. The bundle also carries a second live activity, NOOPLiveActivity, for live heart rate: it is a live activity and not a widget family, so it is outside this sheet's eight and outside the count. Accessory families (Lock Screen circular, rectangular and inline) are out of this wave. Three of these eight would reduce cleanly — heart rate, energy and rings — and none of the other five would; that is a decision worth taking on its own rather than inside this one.
