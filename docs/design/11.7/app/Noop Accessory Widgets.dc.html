WAVE 5B The accessory families the five that do not reduce, and the mark that stands in

On the Lock Screen the set loses its colour. All three rules go with it.

Accessory widgets render monochrome. Not dimmer — colourless. Everything in the eight that carries meaning in hue stops carrying it here: amber no longer means needs attention, periwinkle no longer marks a sentence a model wrote, and blue no longer means live. Three of the eight reduce cleanly because their meaning was always in a figure. These five did not, and the answer is not a smaller version of each — it is deciding, placement by placement, which of them has something honest to say in the space and which does not.

WHAT COLOUR HAS TO BECOME Words, in every case, or nothing. Stress cannot lean on its strip, so it says the state. The coach brief cannot lean on periwinkle, so it is labelled — and a label that has to be read is a reason to leave prose out of the small placements altogether. Nothing here gets an alpha value doing a job a word should do: on somebody's photograph, a 40% tint is not a meaning, it is a legibility problem.
TEN PLACEMENTS FILLED, FIVE DECLINED Five families × three placements is fifteen. Ten are drawn. Five are refused in writing, with the reason, because a refused placement is a decision somebody has to be able to look up — otherwise it comes back as a bug report. A circular that shows only the mark is not a widget, it is a shortcut wearing one, and the Lock Screen already has a way to launch an app.
A0 The accessory mark closed ring · no core · monochrome only A closed ring at the stroke the family already uses, and no core. The core is the first thing to go at this size — 18% of a 16 pt glyph is under 3 pt of ink, which in a vibrant render is a grey speck that reads as dirt on the screen. This breaks a rule the icon set states out loud — open ring reads, closed ring acts — so the rule is scoped rather than quietly bent. It governs the app icon set, where Breathe sits beside the instrument in Appearance and the difference has to survive 29 pt. Nothing here sits beside Breathe. With nothing to be confused with, the distinction has no work to do, and closing the ring buys back the legibility the core cost. It is a label glyph and never a content slot. It leads the inline line and labels the circular. It never appears alone.

FOR THE BUILD Five things to hold to
The mark is closed and coreless, and only here. It is not a sixth app icon and it does not go in the Appearance picker. The icon set's open-ring-with-core mark stays exactly as make_icon.py draws it. Build the ring at the family's own 0.135 stroke so it sits in the same family as the app icon, with a floor of 1.5 pt. Below about 14 pt of glyph the ring stops being a ring and the placement should use text alone. A declined placement is not an unimplemented one. Five are refused on this sheet with reasons. Do not register them — a family that offers a placement it cannot fill honestly is worse than one that offers fewer. Nothing here relies on hue. The renderer will take it away. Where the eight used colour to mean something, these say it. If a state cannot be said in the words the space allows, the placement is declined rather than approximated. Empty faces follow the eight, not this sheet. Every rule about the empty state is already drawn in the Aura set and holds unchanged: state what was last seen and when, and never a dash pair where a figure would be.
