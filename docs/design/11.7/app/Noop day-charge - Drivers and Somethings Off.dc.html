The negative finding — first one in the pack

A tick beside a thing that did not happen is a new mark in this app, and it needs to read as reassurance rather than as a checklist you failed. Hence: auraLight ticks, not green — green is the labs hue and means in-band; here nothing is being graded. The five confounders are fixed and always the same five, in the same order, so the card is comparable day to day.

It carries the confidence chip for the whole signal — Good baseline, in the charge hue. Below a trusted baseline there is no chip, because there is no signal: see Silent.

Never — and Raised is where it is hardest

No condition is named, ever. No fever, no infection, no virus, no you may be getting sick. The register is a nudge to rest, and it has to survive the state where the pull to stronger language is strongest. The drawn copy at Raised is the ceiling: “further from your normal than they have been in a month. Take today easy and go to bed early.”

No banner, no system notification, no red. The current shipping rule fires one notification a day and is the regression this replaces — a drain row in a ledger cannot cry wolf.
