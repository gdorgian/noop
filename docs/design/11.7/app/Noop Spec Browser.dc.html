3 · Header + back
Insets 56 / 18 / 8 / 18, gap 12. Back button 34 circle, fill white 6%, border 0.5 white 9%. The chevron is 9×9, drawn from 1.6 pt left + bottom borders rotated 45°, offset x −2.
Two things go wrong here: the label is the parent screen’s name, not this screen’s; and the chevron is drawn, not chevron.left — the SF Symbol is a different weight, a different optical size, and it shrinks with Dynamic Type. This one never shrinks.
Home screens do not use this header. Today’s is 58 / 20 / 4, gap 14, top-aligned.

4 · Toggle
Track 46×28 radius 14. Knob 24 circle #EDF1EF at top/left 2, travelling 18. Off track is white 13%; on is the hue of the surrounding context — aura for the strap, blush for identity, green for Apple Health, lavender for sleep.
Two curves on one control: track 220 ms cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1), knob 240 ms cubic-bezier(.34,1.25,.64,1) with a slight overshoot. A stock Toggle gets neither, and that single detail is most of why the build’s controls feel generic.

8 · Tab bar
Row gap 3, padding 6, radius 26, fill rgba(23,28,26,.82) over a 20 pt blur, border 0.5 white 9%, shadow 0 8 26 rgba(0,0,0,.5). Active tab flex 1.7, filled aura, icon + label. Centre + is a 44 circle with its own aura shadow.
Not .ultraThinMaterial — it carries its own tint and a much wider radius, which lightens the bar and desaturates what is behind it. And the container must be non-interactive with only the row taking touches, or it eats scrolls that start near the bottom.
