Today
+

One session, one reason. Then the app gets out of the way.

Act 3 is the only place Noop asks anything of you, so it asks once. Today's session is a single card with a single recommendation and the reason it changed — breathing rate came in above its zone, so the intervals became a steady ride. Everything after that is either doing it or reading what it cost.

T1 · NEW
Today's session Name, minutes, the zone to hold, the load it adds — and, when the plan changed, what it was before. Underneath: the three readings that decided it, straight out of Acts 1 and 2. Turn off the adapted tweak to see the unadjusted version.
T2 · NEW
Choose something else Five alternatives, each carrying its honest fit for today. The hard one is still tappable — amber means a cost, not a lock. Pick it and the session card, the ready screen and the live screen all become that session.
T3 · NEW
Ready — the promise before the effort The zone ladder with your target lit, and three plain statements about what the watch will and will not do: one buzz if you drift out, auto-pause at the lights, no ring to close. Then one button.
T4 · NEW
In the effort — the orb's grammar, again One number dead centre, beating, with one word underneath — exactly the breathing orb, so the two live screens in the app read the same way. The zone sits below with your dot in it; drift out and the number, the dot and the whole screen warm to amber together. Tap the pause circle.
T5 · NEW
Intervals — the step owns the screen Work or easy, the seconds left, and what is next. Your pulse stays present but demoted to a chip, because during a minute of work the only thing you can act on is the clock. It is running: watch it roll into the easy step.
T6 · NEW
After — priced in Act 1's currency Four figures, your pulse drawn against the zone it was asked to hold, where the effort went, and then the part most apps leave out: what it cost — minutes added to tonight's sleep need, when you are recovered, load added to the week.
T7 · NEW
Every session, in aggregate The across-time view Act 3 never had — how much did I ride this month. Act 9's range control, in Act 3's hue and in the same position: under the header, above the first card. Every figure is summed out of one 258-day record, so the windows are real — and 1Y is dimmed and cannot be picked, because your history does not reach back that far. It is priced in the body-state currency like everything else, it counts the days you did not train, and it keeps no calorie total. Each row carries the source it came from: the strap knows only the days since you paired it, and everything older arrived through the import hub.
Where the + went

Act 2 gave the + to logging. It keeps it — with Start a session as the last tile in that sheet. Here the + jumps straight to the picker so you can reach these screens without rebuilding the sheet.

Night shift, same act

Flip schedule and the session is placed against your shift, not the clock: before it, between sleeps, or on a rest day the shift already spent. The recommendation is the same logic — only the words for today change.
