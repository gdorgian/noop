WAVE 5 The live heart rate activity NOOPLiveActivity — one reading, four presentations

The strap streams. The Lock Screen does not.

This is the sibling of the lift activity and it takes the same shape: four slots in the same order, the same two inks, no controls. One fact makes it a different problem. A live activity gets an update budget, not a stream, so the figure on the Lock Screen is always a little behind the wrist. Every decision on this sheet follows from that — the reading carries its age, there is no trace, and when the strap stops reporting the number leaves rather than sitting there looking current.

NO TRACE, AND THAT IS THE WHOLE POINT The obvious drawing here is a sparkline, and it is wrong. A trace implies a sample per pixel; this card may get a handful of updates a minute. Drawing thirty seconds of line from four points is an invention, and an invention shaped like a measurement is the one thing this app does not do. So: one figure, one zone, and the age of the reading in plain words. If the wearer wants the curve it is two taps away in the app, where the data actually exists.
ONE DIVERGENCE FROM THE LIFT ACTIVITY: AMBER The lift activity never goes amber, and this one does. Amber means needs attention everywhere in the app, and going over the ceiling you set is the single thing this activity has to say that the wearer asked to be told. It is the only amber on the Lock Screen and it is earned. If the ceiling is off, this state never renders — which is the test that keeps it from becoming decoration.

FOR THE BUILD Five things to hold to
The bpm comes off the activity. Engineering confirmed it, and it is the whole reason these agree: the figure here and the figure on the today orb are one value, read once. Two reads of the same stream drift, and they drift silently until a wearer has both on screen. Every reading carries its age. Seconds while it is fresh, then minutes. Never a bare number — a bare number is a claim about right now that a budgeted update cannot support. No reading means no figure. The element is not rendered; the status line takes the width and says what the last value was and when. Not greyed, not dashed, not held. Same rule as the lift activity's numbers slot, for the same reason. Amber only over the ceiling, and only if the ceiling is on. One attention state, gated on a setting the wearer turned on themselves. If Buzz if I go over is off, this face never renders and the card stays blue at 179 bpm. No controls. No stop, no start a session, no mark. Tapping opens the app. The activity is a readout, and the strap keeps reading whether this card exists or not.
