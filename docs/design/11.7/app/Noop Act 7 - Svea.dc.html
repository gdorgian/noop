A generated sentence must never look like a measurement.

Svea is the one feature that leaves the phone, and the one that can be confidently wrong. So the act is built around a single rule made visible: periwinkle is written, bordered monospace tiles are measured. Nothing in between exists. The second rule is that the coach is allowed to say no — a decline is designed as a considered answer, not a failure state.

{{ c.k }}
T1 · NEW
Two materials, never blended Generated prose sits on periwinkle with a “written, not measured” tag. Every figure it leans on is lifted out into a bordered tile in monospace, labelled measured. The chart inside a reply is framed the same way, with the honest caption: Svea chose the window, not the numbers.
T2 · NEW
The brief carries its sources Four chips under the brief are exactly what went into it. Not a “learn more”, not a provenance screen one tap away — the ingredients sit under the dish. If a grant is off, the chip is absent and the copy says so.
T3 · NEW
A proposal is not a plan Put it in today · Move · Skip, and until one is tapped nothing exists. Tapping shows the resolved state with an Undo, so accepting is as cheap as declining. Try the buttons — the card keeps its answer.
T4 · NEW
The decline, designed Ask brief me on last night in the prototype. The refusal is calm, in Svea's own colour, names the missing 2h 30m, explains what a guess would cost you, and offers three things it can do. No amber, no icon of alarm, no retry button — retrying would be the wrong instinct to teach.
T5 · NEW
Consent shown as the payload The permissions screen ends with the actual prompt, and ungranted lines are struck through in it. A toggle is abstract; a struck-through line in the thing that gets sent is not. Flip the journal grant and watch the request change.
T6 · 4a
Hold to speak, and the audio stays here One bar, four states — idle, listening, turning it into text, did not catch that. Speech becomes text on the phone, so the recording is never a file and never leaves; what goes out is the sentence, after you have read it. Hold the mic in the prototype: a hold of under 0.7s lands in did not catch that, which is exactly where the instruction belongs.
T7 · 4a
The brief arrives; it does not interrupt A dateline, then the brief, inside the conversation rather than bolted above it. One case is drawn because one case exists: it lands into an empty thread. Mid-question, it waits — and it does not badge itself while waiting, because a coach that talks over you is not a coach.
T8 · 4a
Ask about a chart — drawn once, placed by rule One affordance, one position: the chart card's footer, trailing edge, after the provenance line. It goes on every chart in Acts 2, 4, 6 and 9 and nowhere else. It attaches the series, not a picture — the values, their dates, the unit — which is why its enable row sits on consent rather than setup. Off is absence, not grey.

CALL
The gate is a feature, not a wall Most people meet Svea with no key. That screen therefore has to do the product's hardest piece of writing: explain why this one feature needs a server, and say plainly that the app is complete without it. It ends with two buttons of equal weight — add a provider, or read what it would be allowed to see first.
