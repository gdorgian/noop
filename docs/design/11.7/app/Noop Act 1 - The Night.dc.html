Act 1 is the only one that changes tomorrow.

Four screens, built on four of the ten templates. Two of them — Tonight and Why last night — don't exist in Noop today, and they're the pair that turns a sleep report into a sleep decision.

T2
Rest — last night Seven sleeps against your own need, not a score. Tap any bar. The stage strip expands into the hypnogram, and tapping Deep or REM picks that stage out of the whole night — the four stages no longer have to be told apart by colour alone.
T10 · NEW
Tonight One recommendation the size of the screen, five bedtimes you can actually pick between, and the two facts that moved it. The only screen in the app that asks for a commitment.
T6 · NEW
Why last night A cause, not a score. Prose first; the four things the night was made of underneath, each one openable. Two of them say “this didn't matter” — that's the point.
T5
Journal — the + button Mood is the first question, drinks the second, everything else a chip. No form, no free text, closes itself. This is the input the whole act reasons about.
T3
Sleep debt The dossier template, first instance. Running balance instead of a deficit alarm, four stats, and your own correlates — this one template now covers the other thirty-one metric screens.
Not everyone sleeps at night

Flip schedule to night shift and the act re-anchors: last sleep rather than last night, an 08:10 bed time against a 15:25 alarm, and a cause list where the enemy is the afternoon heat and a 04:40 coffee. Nothing in the act is anchored to darkness — it is anchored to your own ninety-sleep pattern.

Rules this act had to obey

No score anywhere in the act. Debt is a balance you pay back, never a deficit to fear. Warm amber is the strongest colour used and it appears once, on room temperature. Every screen ends in a verb — decide it, take this into tonight, save — and the estimate that can't be measured says so out loud.
