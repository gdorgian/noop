Noop Design language · v1

You said Whoop is too complicated. So the home screen has no score on it. Your body gets one colour, one sentence, and one instruction — everything numeric lives one tap deeper, where you actually went looking for it.

The rule One number per screen, maximum. If a second number wants in, it has to earn a screen of its own.
Colour is meaning Not traffic lights — a temperature. Cool sage is restored, warm clay is depleted. Nothing goes red at a health-anxious person.
Voice Svea tells you what to do in one line, then offers the receipt. Never “HRV 45% over baseline” as an opener.
Kept from your app Charge · Effort · Rest as the three pillars, session accept/change, Svea. Dropped: three rings side by side, source badges, “Synthesis”.
2Aura in the dark, with the reference vocabulary folded in
2aAura · dark — tick gauge around the orb, dot-column variability, sleep bars with a personal average, a strain slider, and a crosshair trend chart. All seven screens, all tappable.
