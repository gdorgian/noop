/* Act 3 */ @keyframes heartglow{0%{opacity:.4}9%{opacity:.78}
  18%{opacity:.5}27%{opacity:.68}42%{opacity:.4}100%{opacity:.4}} Opacity only — there is no scale on the glow. Six stops, asymmetric: it fires, falls, fires again softer, then rests flat from 42% to 100%. That is a double beat, and it deliberately mirrors the sibling @keyframes heartbeat (which is the scale, applied to the number, not the glow) on the same stops. A two-stop sine gives a pumping blob instead of a cardiac flicker. The two acts differ slightly on purpose — Act 3 sits a touch dimmer. Behind the heart glyph on the pulse row, and behind the live bpm number at .28. In the live bar it is 210×150 at .3 and runs at .52s — one beat cycle. Freeze the animation when paused, do not hide it.

One rule for all five Every glow is one accent colour at low alpha — #17A2E6 as rgba(23,162,230,…), or rgba(242,180,92,…) when the app is being cautious. There is no white glow, no second hue, and no glow on anything the user cannot act on. If a new element needs one, it is because it is live or it is the one thing to tap.
